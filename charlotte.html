Board Member

Charlotte Lee

President & Co-Founder

Charlotte is a co-founder of From Hooks to Hope and helps lead the organization’s vision, outreach, and chapter development. She is passionate about combining creativity with service and helping students use handmade work to make a meaningful difference in their communities.

Through From Hooks to Hope, she works to expand access to crochet-based service opportunities while building a stronger network of student leaders committed to compassion, comfort, and community impact.
