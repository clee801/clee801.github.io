Charlotte Lee

Director of Operations & Co-Founder

Charlotte is a co-founder of From Hooks to Hope and oversees operations, including managing chapter applications and supporting new and existing chapters.

She is passionate about helping students bring handmade comfort to pediatric patients through organized, meaningful service projects.

She is currently a junior at Walter Payton College Prep in Chicago and has been crocheting since 2016.
