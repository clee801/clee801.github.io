From Hooks to Hope is a student-led nonprofit founded in Chicago
that donates handmade crochet plushies to children in hospitals.

Our Mission

From Hooks to Hope is a student-founded nonprofit dedicated to creating handmade plush items for pediatric patients. By designing and crocheting each item entirely by hand, we partner with hospitals to provide comfort and emotional support to children during hospital stays while connecting crocheters with communities in need.

How It Works

From Hooks to Hope turns handmade creativity into meaningful comfort through a simple, community-centered process.

01

We Crochet

Our members design and crochet handmade plushies with care, creativity, and comfort in mind.

02

We Organize

We collect finished plushies, coordinate donation efforts, and prepare each project for hospital partners.

03

We Donate

We deliver plushies to pediatric patients so each child receives something handmade and heartfelt.
