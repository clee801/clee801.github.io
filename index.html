Stitching Compassion Into Every Loop

From Hooks to Hope is a student-led organization that connects crocheters with communities in need by creating handmade donations for shelters, hospitals, and people who need warmth and comfort.

Our Mission

We bring together volunteers who crochet and craft handmade items to support communities in need.
