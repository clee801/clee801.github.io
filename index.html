From Hooks to Hope is a student-led nonprofit founded in Chicago
that donates handmade crochet plushies to children in hospitals.

Why We Do It

Hospital stays can feel overwhelming and isolating. A small handmade object can offer comfort, familiarity, and a reminder that someone cares.
