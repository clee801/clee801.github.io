Board Member

Mika Matsuyama

Director of Expansion & Co-Founder

Mika is a co-founder of From Hooks to Hope and, as Director of Expansion, supports the organization’s service initiatives and helps expand its impact to new chapters.

She plays a key role in outreach, ensuring handmade pieces reach children in a way that feels personal and meaningful.

Mika is currently a student at Walter Payton College Prep in Chicago and has been crocheting since 2024.
