Our Story

From Hooks to Hope began with something simple: a crochet hook, a ball of yarn, and a group of students who loved creating by hand.

What started as a hobby grew into a mission to bring comfort to pediatric hospital patients through handmade plush toys.

A Shared Hobby

For Charlotte, Vivian, and Mika, crochet began as something we did after school in quiet, creative moments.

Over time, it became something more meaningful — a way to create comfort through handmade work.

Building a Community

We revived our school’s crochet club and quickly saw it grow into a space for learning, collaboration, and friendship.

Beginners and experienced members worked together to create something bigger than ourselves.

Our First Impact

During our first major drive, we created 33 handmade plush animals and donated them to Lurie Children’s Hospital of Chicago.

The Moment That Defined Us

When a child received a handmade plush fox and immediately lit up, we understood the real impact of what we were creating.

That moment became the foundation of From Hooks to Hope.

Growing the Mission

We are now expanding into a larger nonprofit, building chapters and partnerships to reach more hospitals and more children.

The Heart of It All

A hook. Yarn. Time.

Small actions, repeated with care, become something powerful.
