Our Story

From Hooks to Hope began with something simple: a crochet hook, a ball of yarn, and a small group of students who loved creating by hand.

What started as a hobby quickly grew into a project focused on bringing comfort to pediatric hospital patients through handmade plush toys.

A Shared Hobby

For the three of us — Charlotte, Vivian, and Mika — crochet started as something we did in quiet moments after school.

The rhythm of crochet — hook, loop, pull through, repeat — became something calming and creative in our daily lives.

Building a Community

During freshman year, we discovered that our school once had a crochet club — but it had disappeared.

Sophomore year, we decided to bring it back.

What started as a hobby group quickly became a space where beginners learned, experienced members helped, and friendships formed.

Our First Impact

During our junior year, we organized our first plushie donation drive.

We created 33 handmade plush animals and partnered with Lurie Children’s Hospital to donate them.

The Moment That Defined Us

A young child received a plush fox — and instantly lit up.

That moment showed us that something small, handmade, and personal could have a powerful emotional impact.

That’s when From Hooks to Hope truly began.

Growing the Mission

We are now expanding into a full nonprofit, building partnerships, supporting volunteers, and growing chapters.

Our goal is to create a nationwide network of crocheters creating comfort.

The Heart of It All

A hook. Yarn. Time.

That’s all it takes to create something meaningful for a child who needs comfort.
