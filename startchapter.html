Interested in starting a chapter?

Help bring handmade comfort to pediatric patients in your own school or community. By starting a From Hooks to Hope chapter, you can build a space where students create together, give back together, and make a direct impact through every plushie made.

What does a chapter do?

Chapters bring together volunteers who crochet plush toys and other handmade items for pediatric hospital patients. Along the way, members learn new skills, organize donation drives, host workshops, and grow a community centered around creativity, compassion, and service.

Whether your members are complete beginners or experienced crocheters, a chapter creates opportunities for everyone to contribute.
