Completely funded by generosity

From Hooks to Hope is a student-led nonprofit that relies entirely on donations from supporters, families, and community members who believe in the power of handmade comfort.

How donations help

Every plush toy we donate is crocheted by volunteers using materials purchased through donations. Your support helps us buy yarn, stuffing, safety eyes, hooks, storage supplies, and other materials needed to create handmade plush toys for pediatric hospital patients.

Donations also help cover packaging, transportation for hospital deliveries, and resources for expanding our volunteer and chapter network.

Every gift, no matter the size, helps us create more moments of comfort, joy, and connection for children in hospitals.
