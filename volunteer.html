Interested in volunteering?

Join a community of students and makers who use crochet to bring comfort to pediatric patients. Whether you are brand new to crochet or already love making plushies, there is a place for you in From Hooks to Hope.

What do volunteers do?

Volunteers help crochet plush toys and other handmade items for pediatric hospital patients. They can also support donation drives, attend group crochet sessions, help organize materials, and contribute to events that grow our impact.

You do not need to be an expert to get involved. Chapters and club members learn together, support one another, and turn creativity into something meaningful for children in hospitals.
