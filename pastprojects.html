Each drive represents the time, creativity, and care our volunteers put into creating handmade plush toys for pediatric patients. As our organization grows, every donation drive will be documented here.

Winter Wonderland Drive

Our first official donation drive focused on creating winter-themed plush toys for pediatric hospital patients. Members of the club spent weeks crocheting handmade animals that were donated to Lurie Children’s Hospital of Chicago.
