Board Member

Vivian Rosado Griffith

Director of Finances & Co-Founder

Vivian is a co-founder of From Hooks to Hope and, as Director of Finance, plays a central role in ensuring all service chapters are equipt with the necessary materials to make a joyful impact on their community.

Through her leadership, she helps provide chapters with the resources needed to bring comfort and joy to children in hospitals while creating meaningful opportunities for students to serve others.

She is currently a student at Walter Payton College Prep in Chicago and has been crocheting since 2022.
