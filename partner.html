Interested in partnering with us?

We work with hospitals to donate handmade plush toys and other crocheted items that bring comfort to pediatric patients. Through each partnership, we hope to create small moments of joy, warmth, and reassurance for children during difficult hospital experiences.

What does a partnership look like?

Partner hospitals can request donations for pediatric patients and connect with our organization about future drives, delivery logistics, and the types of handmade items that would be most helpful for their patients.

As From Hooks to Hope grows, we aim to build lasting hospital partnerships that allow us to serve more children and respond thoughtfully to the needs of each community we work with.
